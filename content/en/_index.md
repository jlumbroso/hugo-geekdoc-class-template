{{< announcement title="Welcome to Penn Engineering" date="2025-01-15" >}}
**Welcome** to ENGR 101, where we explore the fundamentals of engineering design and innovation. This course embodies Penn Engineering's commitment to **Reinventing Invention** — preparing the next generation of responsible innovators to expand the frontiers of knowledge.

**The spark of ingenuity happens here.** Throughout this semester, you'll engage with transdisciplinary collaboration, hands-on projects, and real-world challenges that define the future of engineering. From robotics to bioengineering, from data science to sustainable systems, we'll explore how engineers shape our world.

**Join us** as we embark on a journey powered by collaboration, charged by transformation, and driven by the relentless pursuit of innovation. Together, we're setting the boundaries of tomorrow by pushing them forward today.
{{< /announcement >}}
